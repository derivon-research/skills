# Derivon Skills

Derivon Skills turn source-backed knowledge into a learning graph whose concepts and derivations remain independently readable without blurring their roles.

## Object Documents

**Concept**:
One reusable understanding state or established claim that can be stated independently of any route used to reach it.
_Avoid_: Topic, chapter, node

**Problem pressure**:
A specific limitation, contradiction, practical obstacle, or discussion burden that makes the current concepts insufficient for the purpose at hand. Its provenance is historical when documented actors faced it, logical when it follows from the theory itself, and pedagogical when a learner needs a new abstraction to discuss or transfer what they know. It remains part of a derivation unless understanding the problem is itself independently learnable and reusable.
_Avoid_: Topic introduction, historical adjacency, generic motivation, mandatory problem point

**Derivation**:
One complete problem-led explanatory step that exposes problem pressure in the jointly required concepts, performs one identifiable resolving move, and establishes one resulting concept.
_Avoid_: Relation, transition, connection, multi-stage historical survey

**Concept document**:
The learner-facing statement and scope of one concept. It may state a result, but does not justify that result from graph prerequisites.
_Avoid_: Proof document, derivation summary

**Derivation document**:
The learner-facing account of the problem pressure, resolving argument or construction, and resulting concept. It shows why all required concepts participate and why the new concept is needed.
_Avoid_: Concept definition, relationship note

**Viewpoint**:
A reusable claim that answers problem pressure, labeled with its author or school and a plain-language statement of the claim rather than a name alone.
_Avoid_: Person, era, unexplained specialist term

**Response family**:
Distinct derivations that share problem pressure but resolve it into different viewpoints or concepts. A later response depends on an earlier viewpoint only when it actually summarizes, refines, rejects, or otherwise reasons from that viewpoint.
_Avoid_: Timeline, school succession, parallel hyperedges with different heads

## Exploration

**Explored knowledge**:
Source-grounded content investigated through an exploration question and recorded in the graph. It may exist before the learner demonstrates usable understanding.
_Avoid_: Mastered knowledge, curriculum roadmap

**Observed understanding**:
Evidence from a non-recall learner task that calibrates explanations, prerequisites, and personal derivation weights. It does not decide whether source-grounded content may exist in the graph.
_Avoid_: Point existence, self-reported familiarity

**Teaching batch**:
The smallest useful group of concepts and derivations that answers one concrete exploration question. A broad learning goal selects questions but does not authorize a roadmap-sized batch.
_Avoid_: Chapter, historical period, complete route

## Publication

**Plainspoken teaching prose**:
Idiomatic prose in the publication language that puts the subject and main claim early and gives each sentence one main teaching job. A plainspoken pass revises every sentence whose subject, main claim, or teaching job is unclear on first reading.
_Avoid_: Concise prose, natural prose, academic compression

## Exploration

**Explored knowledge**:
Source-backed concepts and derivations investigated with the learner and included in the Mindmap whether or not the learner has demonstrated mastery.
_Avoid_: Mastered knowledge, curriculum roadmap

**Mastery evidence**:
Evidence from learner performance that calibrates teaching decisions and cognitive-cost observations without determining whether source-backed knowledge may exist in the graph.
_Avoid_: Graph membership, self-reported familiarity

**Exploration batch**:
The smallest source-backed graph and document change that answers one concrete learner question, normally one derivation with its necessary concepts or one bounded response family.
_Avoid_: Chapter import, speculative roadmap expansion
